# Shipping Staging-First

We switched to a staging-first workflow a few months ago. The basic shape:

- `feature/*` branches open pull requests against `staging`
- CI runs on every push to `staging`
- A human promotes `staging` to `main` after each integration cycle

## What changed

Reviewers started looking at the *interaction* between PRs, not just the diff at hand. Bugs that only manifested when two features landed back-to-back got caught before they reached `main`.

## What didn't

The promotion step still needs a human. We haven't found a way to safely automate it.

## What we'd tweak

Make the staging workflow louder in onboarding docs.
