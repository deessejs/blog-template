# Designing for Readers

The best blog posts keep one reader in mind: a busy person who landed on your page from a link and wants to know if it's worth their time.

## Lead with the takeaway

Open with the conclusion. If the reader leaves after the first paragraph, they should still have the main idea.

## Cut the throat-clearing

Skip "In this article, we will explore…" — just start.

## Use concrete examples

Abstract advice is forgettable. A specific instance sticks.
